INTRO SUBTITLE ANIMATION / TRANSITION FIX
==========================================

Fixed frame allocation for short intro durations. Entrance and exit effects
now receive separate, non-overlapping frame ranges, including 1-second and
2-second intros. Previously, both effects could claim the same complete frame
range and the exit transition could be skipped.

Intro subtitle rendering continues to apply the configured Unicode font style,
selected animation, selected transition, and text/image positions to the
generated transparent overlay. Invalid animation/transition names safely
fall back to None.
